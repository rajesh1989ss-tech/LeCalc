Help & printing

Printing

Reports always print on a white background with black text, whatever theme you use on screen.

Chrome adds its own header and footer — the URL, the date and the page number. Turn them off once in the print dialogue: More settings → Headers and footers. You have to do this on desktop Chrome and again on Android Chrome; the setting is remembered per device.

Android's print flow ignores some page rules and renders from a snapshot, so check the first page before saving a long report as PDF. Desktop Chrome is the reliable one for multi-page reports.

Keyboard (desktop)

0-9 . + - * / ( ) — type into the pad · Enter = equals · Backspace = delete · Esc = clear · h = history · % = percent

Percent

200+10% gives 220 — the percentage is taken of the number before it. A percentage on its own, or after × and ÷, is simply the number divided by 100.

Formulas

  • m³ ⇆ MT — MT = m³ × density; m³ = MT ÷ density.
  • Quick ETC — remaining = total − done; hours = remaining ÷ rate.
  • Line displacement — V = π/4 × (inches × 0.0254)² × length in metres. 1 m³ = 6.28981 bbl.
  • Flow rate — Q = π/4 × (inches × 0.0254)² × velocity × 3600.
  • Tank rinsing — per tank = output × machines × minutes ÷ 60, fixed plus portable; total = per tank × tanks.
  • Interpolation — Y = Y₁ + (X − X₁) × (Y₂ − Y₁) ÷ (X₂ − X₁).
  • Cargo operation — rate comes from the last two figures, over active time only; stopped time is excluded from every rate and ETC.
